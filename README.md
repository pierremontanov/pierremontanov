### My Approach

Most companies don't need another platform. They need the platform they already have to do more.

I specialize in designing and building **backend AI services** that plug into existing systems. No migrations, no vendor lock-in, no disruption. Think of it as adding an intelligence layer to infrastructure that already works.

```
  Business Need          What I Deliver           Your System
  ─────────────          ──────────────           ───────────
  "We need to            Requirements > API >     EHR now auto-imports
   process 200            OCR pipeline >           structured patient
   PDFs a day"            Docker deploy            data in seconds

  Stakeholders ──▶  BA + Engineer (same person) ──▶  Production
```

I bring a dual perspective to every project: the **business analyst** who maps stakeholder needs, compliance requirements, and ROI, and the **engineer** who builds the API, writes the tests, and ships the container.

**[DocIQ](https://github.com/pierremontanov/HealthParse)** | A backend AI engine that converts unstructured medical PDFs (scanned or digital, English or Spanish) into clean, validated, FHIR-compliant JSON. Built to plug into existing healthcare infrastructure without replacing anything.

Prescriptions, lab results, clinical histories: in as a PDF, out as structured data your EHR, LIMS, or patient portal already understands.

**Stack:** Python, FastAPI, Tesseract OCR, PaddleOCR, EasyOCR, OpenCV, Pydantic v2, Regex, FHIR R4, Docker &nbsp;|&nbsp; **1,100+ tests** &nbsp;|&nbsp; Production-ready with Nginx, rate limiting, and health probes.
